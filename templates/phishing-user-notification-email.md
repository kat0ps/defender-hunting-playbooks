# Template: Phishing Notification to Recipients

Send to everyone who received a malicious email (phishing triage, step 3). Keep it short and non-technical.

**Subject:** Security notice: phishing email removed from your mailbox

Hi all,

On **<date>** you received an email that we've confirmed as **phishing**:

- **From:** <display name> <sender address>
- **Subject:** "<subject>"

We've removed it from all mailboxes.

**If you didn't click anything:** no action is needed. Thank you for staying alert.

**If you clicked the link, opened the attachment or entered your password:** please contact <IT/SOC contact, phone or Teams> **straight away**. Don't wait, and don't worry about being in trouble. Reporting quickly is what protects you and the company.

**Remember:**
- Check the sender's real address, not just the display name.
- Be suspicious of urgent requests about payments, passwords or banking details.
- Use the **Report** button in Outlook for anything that looks suspicious.

Thank you to everyone who reported this email.

Kind regards,
<Name>
<Team / SOC>
